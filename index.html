How it works

Three steps, no change to your kitchen routine beyond separating food waste.

1. Separate

Your staff put food waste into the cooled Humus Coast bin. Bin, washing and fill sensor are included.

2. Collect and weigh

We collect on the hygiene schedule and weigh every pickup, so you always know your tonnage.

3. Compost

Your food waste becomes compost for local farms at our site in Kaštela.

Frequently asked questions

How often do you collect?
With our cooled bins: three times a week from May to October and once a week from November to April, as the hygiene rules for food waste require.
Why not a cheaper service with one pickup a week?
From May to October one pickup a week does not meet the hygiene rules, even with a cooled bin. Our price includes a compliant schedule all year.
Are the savings guaranteed?
Savings depend on your volume. For hotels matching the included tonnage, total yearly food-waste cost is at least 15% lower than mixed waste at the 2027 rate. Old-town restaurants on Shared Basic pay about the same as today; Shared Premium costs about €17 a month more and adds the sticker.
What if we produce more food waste than included?
We weigh every pickup. Tonnes above your plan’s included amount cost €190 per tonne, settled once a year.
Is Humus Coast climate neutral?
We do not make that claim. We report what we can prove: the weight of every pickup and the CO₂ figure calculated with a certified method.
