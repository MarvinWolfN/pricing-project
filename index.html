Turn Your Food Waste into Value

We collect separated food waste from your hospitality business and turn it into high-quality compost. By diverting food waste from standard municipal bins, you save significantly on rising waste fees.

See Our Plans

Main Benefits

  • Save money on rising municipal mixed-waste fees.
  • Turn your food waste into valuable compost.
  • Get certified CO2 reporting for your eco-labels.

How it Works

  1. You separate food waste in our provided hygienic bins.
  2. We collect the waste according to strict hygiene schedules.
  3. We turn it into compost and provide your eco-report.
